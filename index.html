About Serendipity

Founded on the belief that true beauty is found in the unexpected, Serendipity has become the destination for brands seeking distinctive faces that captivate and inspire.

Our curated roster represents the pinnacle of fashion modeling, each talent selected not just for their striking features, but for their ability to tell stories through their presence.

We work with the world's most prestigious fashion houses, luxury brands, and visionary photographers to create imagery that transcends trends and defines eras.
